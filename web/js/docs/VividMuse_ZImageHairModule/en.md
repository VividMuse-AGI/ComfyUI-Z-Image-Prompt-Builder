# Z-Image Hair

Builds hair color, undertone, coloring technique, length, texture, hairstyle, bangs, and head-accessory descriptions.

Choose **Basic Hair Color** for a compact workflow. **Advanced Hair Coloring** reveals undertone and coloring-technique controls. Choose **None** for an unwanted field; the prompt will omit it.

Chain the Chinese and English inputs/outputs separately. Use `Ctrl+B` to bypass the module.
