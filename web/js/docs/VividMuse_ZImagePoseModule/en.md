# Z-Image Pose & Action

Builds a coordinated action chain from captured moment, base pose, body orientation, weight distribution, shoulders, hands, legs, head direction, gaze, and expression.

For reliable anatomy, prefer a clear base pose and one readable action per limb. The built-in randomizer selects compatible pose bundles rather than independently mixing every limb.

Chain the Chinese and English inputs/outputs separately. Use `Ctrl+B` to bypass the module.
