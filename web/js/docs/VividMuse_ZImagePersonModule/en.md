# Z-Image Person

Builds age, ethnicity, regional appearance, face, eyes, skin, makeup, and body-shape descriptions.

Use a specific age range rather than only “adult” when age consistency matters. **Ethnicity Group** controls the compatible **Regional Appearance** choices. Makeup supports either a complete preset or individual custom fields.

Chain the Chinese and English inputs/outputs separately. Use `Ctrl+B` to bypass the module.
